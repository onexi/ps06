## Possible Improvements
If implemented, prioritize the sourced brief, three lessons, and tutor before commercial workflow automation. Recruit pilot learners early and keep demand claims separate from recruited participation; learning assessment and failure recovery need time within the four-week schedule.

## Distinctive Strength
The plan measures learning, tutor accuracy, recovery, and human intervention rather than treating generated course content as proof of success.
