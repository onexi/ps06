## Overall
Life Without Driving addresses a meaningful mobility problem through a shared, evidence-based journey engine.

## Possible Improvements
Recast the five-week plan around a four-week fixed-route pilot. Validate outbound, visit, and return timing on a few cases first; keep on-demand integration optional and simplify workspaces if needed. Distinguish modeled timetable feasibility from actual booking availability.

## Distinctive Strength
Testing complete outings and reporting both helped and harmed journeys makes the scenario comparison valuable to residents and planners.
