## Overall
TS-Cov is a focused research proposal with an appropriate commitment to reproducibility and reporting negative findings.

## Possible Improvements
Confirm dataset access, timestamped context, and numerical-model covariate compatibility in week one. Freeze a small task panel and call budget; select context using earlier validation windows rather than held-out outcomes, and reserve time for fair baseline comparisons.

## Distinctive Strength
The common evaluation protocol, context-selection baselines, and explicit leakage audit make the research question testable.
