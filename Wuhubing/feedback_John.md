## Overall
ReproScout is a substantive research project with a useful deliverable even if the lessons loop does not improve results.

## Possible Improvements
Pilot sandbox installation and measure actual run time before committing to 25 tasks and roughly 80 runs. Keep a smaller completed benchmark as a fallback, verify model access and budget assumptions, and freeze lessons before held-out evaluation.

## Distinctive Strength
Enforced budgets, isolated answer keys, evidence-linked reports, and explicit failure labels make the experiment unusually inspectable.
