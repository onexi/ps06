## Overall
A well-bounded retail simulation with a serious emphasis on evaluating agent decisions.

## Possible Improvements
Review a pilot set of checkpoints before committing to all 128, and split by conversation to avoid leakage. Include a few complete conversations in the core tests: correct isolated decisions do not establish successful resolution. Judge labels against policy rather than model agreement alone.

## Distinctive Strength
The distinction between recorded representative actions and correct policy decisions is an especially strong evaluation insight.
