## Overall
Travel Atlas offers a clear personal workflow and a useful separation between metadata and editable AI suggestions.

## Possible Improvements
Prioritize import, persistent trips, review, and one recap format; make video the first optional item. Test missing EXIF, time zones, and file handling early, and use held-out photos for location-error and task-time comparisons. Keep inferred places visibly uncertain until confirmed.

## Distinctive Strength
The explicit provenance of metadata versus AI suggestions gives users control over how their memories are represented.
