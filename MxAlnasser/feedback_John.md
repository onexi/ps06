## Overall
World Wide Search tackles a meaningful discovery problem with an appropriately narrow domain pilot.

## Possible Improvements
Confirm multilingual search access and coverage before building the orchestration layer. Clarify whether the MVP has two connectors total or two plus a scholarly index, and define reviewer judgments and a fixed baseline for missed-source and setup-time comparisons.

## Distinctive Strength
Visible query expansion and reproducible multilingual search trails make broader discovery auditable.
