## Overall

Your proposal turns a large policy interest into a bounded evidence-processing project. I liked that you clearly separate the research motivation from what this prototype can establish, and that the output includes the value, unit, year, and source page together.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd make the agent's added contribution concrete with one example where it has to choose between extraction actions or resolve a gap. That will help distinguish its role from the Python baseline you're already planning. For evaluation, reserve some observations or document sections that weren't used to tune the workflow, and report extraction quality before human correction as well as the quality of approved records. Otherwise a careful reviewer could make both approaches look equally accurate while hiding very different amounts of manual work.

## Distinctive Strength

The evidence ledger is a strong choice because it preserves the context needed to interpret each number. A correct value with the wrong year or unit can still mislead a reader. Your proposed exact-match checks and explicit missing status address that problem directly without promising a national impact study.
