## Overall

You describe a specific audience and a real moment of use: trying to make sense of a package or menu while deciding what to eat. I liked the attention to portions, baseline symptoms, and individual experience rather than treating every user as having the same food history.

## Could Be Stronger

The proposal milestones, and a test plan could be stronger. Scanning packages, interpreting plated meals, learning personal patterns, and checking store inventory are several substantial projects. I'd start with barcode or confirmed ingredient input, a source-linked explanation, and a simple diary, leaving meal photos and live inventory for later. Define a small labeled set for checking ingredient extraction and explanation support, and specify what the app should do when information is incomplete. The evaluation should distinguish correctly recording a person's experience from establishing that a particular ingredient caused a symptom.

Be careful, the data collection could be challenging. Make sure to manage scope and time.  

## Distinctive Strength

Having the user confirm the food and ingredients before saving the result is a thoughtful part of the workflow. It gives you a chance to correct an input error before it becomes part of the person's history and influences later suggestions.
