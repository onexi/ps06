## Overall
A thoughtful research design with strong evidence requirements and a well-defined legal research workflow.

## Possible Improvements
The full workspace and three evaluation conditions create a substantial implementation and evaluation workload within four weeks. Secure case access and reviewer availability immediately, and trim interface features to protect time for retrieval and evaluation.

## Distinctive Strength
The design separates relevance from favorable outcomes, seeks contrary cases, and ties comparisons to inspectable source passages.
