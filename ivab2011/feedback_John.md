## Overall
A valuable concept with a sensible staged fallback, but the full proposed scope is ambitious for four weeks.

## Possible Improvements
Verifying 50 regulatory entries, eight themes, and readiness judgments creates a substantial research and validation workload within four weeks. Prioritize a small crosswalk checked against official texts and one synthetic scenario; make tracker expansion and additional views optional.

## Distinctive Strength
Action-specific autonomy levels, explicit legal-status labels, and human approval make the proposed assessment framework distinctive.
