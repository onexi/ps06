## Overall

XFAIR makes an abstract governance problem concrete through Maria's water-utility example. I liked the focus on separate actions and autonomy levels, which gives the proposed assessment a more useful shape than a single readiness score for an entire agent.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd narrow the first implementation to one use case and a small set of control themes, with a clear minimum demonstration before expanding the tracker. Fifty entries, several frameworks, a crosswalk, and readiness reports are a lot to validate while building the software. I'd also strengthen the independent check of the readiness rules. Agreement with your own judgment on four variants is a starting point, but use a second reviewer and cases where evidence is missing or conflicting. Show the source passage, the interpretation, and the rule that produces each result so a reviewer can challenge the mapping.

## Distinctive Strength

Labeling links as binding, interpretive, voluntary, or unresolved is a thoughtful way to expose uncertainty. That distinction is central to the value of this project: a source-backed recommendation should make its reasoning inspectable instead of making every mapping look equally authoritative.
