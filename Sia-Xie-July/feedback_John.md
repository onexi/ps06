## Overall
StarCrew combines a compelling personal use case with unusually concrete data feasibility checks.

## Possible Improvements
Replace the six-week schedule with a four-week core: solo planning, a small site list, and one re-planning demonstration. Add group logistics only after that works. Backtests must use forecasts available at decision time; historical observed weather alone cannot validate forecast-based choices.

## Distinctive Strength
Separating deterministic astronomy and constraint checks from LLM reasoning gives the recommendations a strong factual foundation.
