## Overall
Read2Discover has a clear user flow and a well-bounded MVP that is credible within four weeks.

## Possible Improvements
Test search quality from both required sources early. Define relevance and diversity labels before tuning, keep evaluation scenarios held out, and leave Bluesky and feedback learning optional until the two-source loop is reliable.

## Distinctive Strength
The two-source limit, single retry, and keyword baseline make the agent's contribution practical to evaluate.
