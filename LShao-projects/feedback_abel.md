## Overall

Your proposal addresses the useful gap between seeing a KPI move and understanding what contributed to the change. The dashboard context and investigation loop fit together well, and the chosen dataset gives you a concrete starting point rather than an open-ended data integration project.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd turn Find, Investigate, and Explain into a short scoring guide with a worked gold case. Specify the expected calculation, acceptable alternative investigation paths, and what counts as an unsupported explanation. In particular, distinguish a segment that accounts for a revenue change from a claim about why customers behaved differently. Your own list of missing data makes that distinction important. Keeping the held-out cases fixed will help you assess the agent without quietly adjusting the questions to its strengths.

## Distinctive Strength

Evaluating the investigation trajectory, not just the final prose, is the strongest part of this plan. A persuasive answer can conceal a wrong join or an unnecessary chain of queries. Logging the steps gives you a way to see whether the agent reached a sound answer for the right reasons.
