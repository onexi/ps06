## Overall
A useful analytics assistant with a strong dataset and an evaluation plan that goes beyond attractive dashboards.

## Possible Improvements
The proposal schedules five weeks; bring a narrow end-to-end investigation forward to fit the four-week deadline. Validate joins and KPI definitions first, cap agent work, and distinguish observed drivers from causal explanations the dataset cannot support.

## Distinctive Strength
The development/held-out case split and full investigation traces provide a strong basis for evaluating evidence quality.
