## Overall
TrueTrip addresses a concrete travel decision with a clear extension-based workflow and a convincing cost comparison.

## Possible Improvements
Prototype Booking card extraction and verify rating API access first. Start with a small hotel sample and one rating source; page changes, hotel matching, and fee verification may take longer than the UI. Distinguish estimates from verified charges.

## Distinctive Strength
Source-linked parking fees, explicit unknowns, and break-even explanations make the total-cost calculation inspectable.
