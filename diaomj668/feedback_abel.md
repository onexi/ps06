Good proposal. Your explanation is clear and effective. In future proposals I would add images. It warms up and humanizes the concept. At the outset I think the challenge will be to balance scope between ambition and being realistic about the timeline.  

## Overall

Your Massachusetts Trip Planner makes the workflow easy to understand: gather records, draft a plan, and check whether the stops fit. I liked that you disclose the age and missing fields in the dataset instead of treating every place as equally well documented.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd make the meaning of a checked plan more precise. With a 2021 snapshot and prices present for only 20% of records, passing the checker cannot establish that a trip is currently open and within budget. Show whether each constraint is verified, estimated, or unknown, and either narrow the demo to a manually checked set of places or clearly frame it as planning against a historical snapshot. Also choose the travel-time method before evaluating time clashes; straight-line distance alone doesn't specify how long the traveler needs between stops.

## Distinctive Strength

The loop that rebuilds a plan when a code check fails is a useful response to the actual planning problem. Showing the checker output alongside source records could help a user understand both why a plan works and which details still need confirmation.
