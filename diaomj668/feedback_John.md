## Overall
The trip planner has a sensible checked-planning core and useful early measurements of data completeness.

## Possible Improvements
Make the one-day planner the committed deliverable and prioritize extensions after evaluation. The 2021 snapshot and sparse prices cannot support current availability or firm budget claims; distinguish missing information from verified constraints and avoid treating straight-line travel as realistic routing.

## Distinctive Strength
Explicit hours, travel, and budget checks plus record-linked stops provide a strong alternative to unchecked itinerary generation.
