## Overall

Activity Pulse starts from a specific problem that matters to a task team: knowing whose action is pending and which document needs attention. Your example makes that workflow easy to follow, and separating the local prototype from future institutional integrations keeps the proposal manageable.

## Could Be Stronger

There isn't a major proposal gap here. I'd strengthen the small synthetic test set by reserving a few unfamiliar email threads for the final check, including ambiguous references, conflicting versions, and messages that don't belong to any activity. With 15–20 emails, report the actual counts alongside percentages so one successful example doesn't look like broad reliability. It would also help to compare the proposed workflow with reference-number matching alone, showing where the model earns its added cost and review effort.

## Distinctive Strength

You explicitly distinguish a request, a commitment, a prediction, and a completed action. That is a thoughtful choice for this setting: an email asking for clearance shouldn't quietly become evidence that clearance happened. Your separate checks for source traceability and meaning carry that distinction into the evaluation.
