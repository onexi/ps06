## Overall
Rent Radar addresses a clear student need, supported by valuable early checks of listing quality and API limits.

## Possible Improvements
Prioritize Boston layers and a clearly defined listing snapshot before Cambridge and extra amenities. Replace the 100% active-listing target with measurable coverage of retrieved records; reports of complaints or crashes need geographic and time context and do not establish personal safety.

## Distinctive Strength
The proposal already identifies stale listings and biased area scores, and commits to showing underlying evidence rather than opaque neighborhood ratings.
