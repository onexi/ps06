I love the idea of your project! I think this is information that a city like Boston could benefit from tremendously. At the outset make sure you can secure enough data sources so that you can create the visualizations that you're looking for.  

## Overall

Rent Radar tackles a practical student problem, and the listing report gives the map a clear purpose beyond showing apartment locations. Your reported data check is especially useful because it identifies stale listings and gaps before the project depends on them.

## Could Be Stronger

Some things to consider. I'd revise the target of showing 100% of active listings. The proposal establishes a sample from one provider, not a complete inventory of the rental market. Define coverage against the records your pipeline can actually retrieve and validate. I'd also show missing geographic coverage explicitly, especially while Cambridge and Boston layers are being added separately. A lack of complaint records should appear as missing or limited evidence, not reassurance that an address has no problems. That distinction matters directly to the apartment decision you're trying to support.

## Distinctive Strength

Your choice to show underlying records instead of labeling whole neighborhoods good or bad is thoughtful. It lets a student inspect the evidence relevant to their own needs. The stale-listing finding also shows why the data pipeline and timestamps deserve as much attention as the agent interface.
