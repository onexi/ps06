Great idea. However, there are several challenges. Make sure you push on those at the outset to minimize your risk.  

## Overall

Signal gives your earlier stock-price work a clear next direction: keeping track of the evidence behind company changes. The one-company midpoint and the option to retain manual refresh make the scope believable.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd define the 12–15 research tasks and their expected answers before building much of the interface. Include cases with different reporting periods, revised figures, and insufficient evidence for an explanation. Score numerical correctness and whether the cited passage supports the claim separately. I'd also make the usefulness comparison more specific: give users the same question and source material, then measure time to a checked answer, including their verification work. That would test your promise of less repeated research more directly than asking whether they prefer a generated brief to a summary.

## Distinctive Strength

Separating verified changes, management explanations, and open questions is the strongest part of the proposed research record. Those are different kinds of evidence. Keeping them distinct should make the brief easier to question and update when a new filing changes what the user knows.
