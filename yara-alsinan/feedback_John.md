## Overall
Signal is a practical research assistant with an achievable one-company starting point and sensible expansion options.

## Possible Improvements
Validate period alignment, units, and amended filings before adding companies or scheduled checks. Keep a manual-refresh fallback, separate management explanations from verified causal findings, and freeze the 12–15 evaluation tasks before tuning.

## Distinctive Strength
The persistent research record and historical-filing replay test directly address repeated work and duplicate updates.
