 I love your idea! It's so relatable and your presentation communicates what you intend to do effectively. Great job.  

## Overall

Kit has a clear personal motivation, and you explain the different needs of your daughter and a parent well. Keeping the first complete flow to a spoken question, a sourced answer, and a separate help request gives the project a useful center.

## Could Be Stronger

I'd name the candidate speech and language models and test the bilingual voice flow on the intended hardware early. Your preliminary synthetic-speech timing is useful, and you correctly leave real child speech unverified. For the evaluation, make quality, privacy, and alerts as concrete as the speed target: specify the question set, what counts as a supported age-appropriate answer, and how missed alerts and false alarms will be recorded. Also spell out which information each parent update contains and what happens if delivery fails, so the privacy promise can be checked against the actual workflow.

## Distinctive Strength

The help button bypassing the model is a thoughtful choice. A child's request to contact a parent shouldn't depend on the model correctly interpreting the conversation first. Checking delivery separately also keeps sending a request from being confused with a parent actually receiving it.
