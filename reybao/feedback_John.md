## Overall
Kit has a clear personal audience and a thoughtful local-first question-and-answer prototype.

## Possible Improvements
Test real child speech in both languages and local-model latency early. Prioritize sourced answers and a direct, delivery-checked parent help request; keep 100 reviewed entries and automated concern detection adjustable. Sensitive-disclosure tests should report misses and false alarms rather than imply reliable protection.

## Distinctive Strength
The separation of model suggestions, rule-controlled actions, and a parent button that bypasses model approval is a strong design choice.
